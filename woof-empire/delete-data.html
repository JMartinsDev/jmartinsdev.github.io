Woof Empire – Idle Dog Clicker: delete your data

Developer: Jonatan Martín (JMartinsDev). Contact: jmartinsjdev@gmail.com.

The game has no user accounts and we do not keep any of your personal data on our own servers. Your data is on your device and, if you use them, in Google services. This is how to delete it:

1. Your game on this device

In the game, open Options → Delete save and confirm. Or uninstall the app. Your progress, achievements, settings and the year of birth you entered (if any) are deleted from the device immediately.

2. Your cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your progress is also saved in Google's cloud. Delete save in the game also replaces that copy with a new empty game. To delete it completely, open the Google Play Games app (or Settings → Google → Play Games on some devices), go to your profile settings and choose to delete the data of Woof Empire (or your whole Play Games account and data). Google deletes it according to its own policies.

3. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads), and change your ad consent in the game in Options → Privacy & ads. Google keeps advertising data according to its retention policy.

4. Ask us

You can also email jmartinsjdev@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data – Woof Empire”. We reply within 30 days and delete any message or data we hold about you (including anything you sent through the feedback form: text and email); we help you with the Google steps if needed.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your game on the device (immediately), your cloud save (when you delete it in Google Play Games) and any email or feedback form submission you sent us (when you ask us or, at the latest, after 12 months).
  • Kept: if you bought the VIP Pass, Google Play keeps the purchase record so the purchase can be restored and to meet legal and tax obligations, according to Google's policies. We do not keep a copy. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its retention policy.
