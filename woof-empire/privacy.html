Woof Empire – Idle Dog Clicker privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026.

This policy explains what data is processed when you play Woof Empire – Idle Dog Clicker ("the game") on Android. The data controller is Jonatan Martín (JMartinsDev), an independent developer based in Spain. Contact: jmartinsjdev@gmail.com.

1. What the game itself stores

The game has no user accounts and does not send us any personal data. Your progress (bones, buildings, achievements, language and settings) is saved on your own device.

Age. To show ads suitable for your age, the game asks Google Play for your age range when Google knows it (Play Age Signals). If it doesn't, the game asks for your year of birth once; it is stored only on your device and is not sent to anyone. Players under 16 only see non-personalized ads suitable for minors; everyone else sees ads with content suitable for teens at most.

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your progress is also saved in Google's cloud, linked to your Play Games account, so it isn't lost when you change phones or reinstall the game. That copy is managed by Google; we have no access to it outside the game.

2. Third-party services

The game uses these Google services, which may collect data from your device under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads). Shows ads, which are always optional except for an occasional one after the Great Walk. It may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), technical device data, ad interactions and diagnostics, to show, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Google Play Games (if you sign in): your Play Games player ID and profile and your saved game.
  • Google Play (purchases): if you buy the VIP Pass, payment is processed by Google. We only receive confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Consent and how to change it

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your permission when you start (using Google's consent form) before using your data for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Options → Privacy & ads. If you don't consent, you may see non-personalized ads or none at all, and the game works the same.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Google → Ads).

4. Legal basis

Personalized ads are based on your consent. Running the game, saving your progress and purchases are based on providing the service you request. Fraud prevention and basic ad measurement are based on legitimate interest.

5. Retention and deletion

Step-by-step instructions: How to delete your data.

  • The save on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or use "Delete save" in Options.
  • The save in Google Play Games can be deleted from the Google Play Games app or settings (delete the game's data or your Play Games account).
  • Data collected by AdMob and Google Play is kept according to Google's policies.
  • We do not keep any of your personal data. If you email us, we keep the message only as long as needed to reply.

6. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at younger children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and players under 16 only see non-personalized ads suitable for minors. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection to processing, and portability of your data, by writing to jmartinsjdev@gmail.com. For data processed by Google services, you can also contact Google. If you are not satisfied, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local authority.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
